Support

One inbox · hello@protocol42.io

ASIScan is built and supported by a small team, which has one advantage worth stating: the person who answers your email is the person who wrote the rule you're asking about.

Before you email

Two things resolve most issues faster than we can reply:

  1. Run npm test. Seventeen tests ship with the Software. If they pass, your install is sound and the problem is likely configuration or a rule question. If they fail, paste the failure into your email — that tells us almost everything.
  2. Check the FAQ. Installation, language support, licensing scope, and the "why did it flag this?" cases are covered there.

What we can help with

Please don't send us your source code

We don't want it, we don't need it, and holding it creates risk for both of us. A rule ID and a redacted one-line snippet is enough to diagnose almost any finding.

Never send credentials, API keys, tokens, or customer data. If a finding involves a real secret, rotate it first — then tell us about the pattern, not the value.

What support does not cover

Being straight about this so expectations are right from the start:

  • We don't audit your code for you. Support covers the tool, not your codebase. Interpreting findings in the context of your architecture is your engineering judgement to exercise.
  • We can't tell you if you're compliant. Not with the EU AI Act, not with anything else. That's a legal determination requiring a qualified professional, and anyone who tells you a scanner settles it is selling you a false sense of security.
  • No guaranteed response time. We aim for one to two business days and usually beat it, but there is no SLA and none is promised. See section 8 of the Terms.
  • Custom rule development isn't included, though we're happy to point you at the right file — rules are plain data objects and most additions are a few lines.

Reporting a security issue in ASIScan itself

If you find a vulnerability in the Software, email hello@protocol42.io with SECURITY in the subject line. Please give us a reasonable window to investigate and ship a fix before public disclosure — we will acknowledge within two business days, keep you updated, and credit you in the release notes if you'd like.

We won't threaten legal action against anyone who reports a genuine vulnerability in good faith.

Updates

Your licence includes all v1.x updates at no charge. Rules move as OWASP publishes and as false-positive reports come in, so updating is worth doing periodically. We'll email licence holders when a release contains a meaningful rule change.
